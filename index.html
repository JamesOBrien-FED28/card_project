Civilization VI

4X Turn-Based Strategy : 2016

A turn-based strategy game where you build and expand an empire from the ancient era to the modern age. You develop cities, research technologies, engage in diplomacy or warfare, and compete with other leaders to achieve one of several victory conditions.

Final Fantasy VII

JRPG : 1997

A story-driven JRPG where Cloud Strife and a group of rebels fight against the megacorporation Shinra and the enigmatic Sephiroth. It blends character-focused drama with exploration, turn-based combat, and a richly atmospheric world.

Nidhogg

2D Fighting Game : 2014

A fast, minimalist fencing duel game where two players battle to reach opposite ends of a side-scrolling arena. Its frantic swordplay and tug-of-war structure create constant tension and hilarious chaos.

Persona 4 Golden

JRPG : 2012

A hybrid JRPG/social sim where a group of teens investigate supernatural murders in a small rural town. You balance dungeon crawling with building relationships that strengthen your party and shape the story.

Fatal Frame 2

Survival Horror : 2003

A survival horror game where twin sisters explore a haunted village filled with restless spirits. You fight ghosts using the Camera Obscura, creating slow-burn tension and deeply atmospheric scares.

Slay the Spire

Roguelite / Deck Builder : 2019

A deck-building roguelike where you climb a shifting tower, crafting card synergies to survive increasingly dangerous encounters. Every run forces strategic choices that dramatically change your playstyle.

Rocket League

Car Football : 2015

A high-speed sports game that mixes arcade driving with soccer, letting players smash rocket-powered cars into a giant ball. Its physics-based skill ceiling makes matches both accessible and endlessly competitive.

Stardew Valley

Farming Sim : 2016

A cozy farming and life-sim where you restore a neglected farm, build relationships, and explore a small rural community. Its open-ended structure encourages relaxation, creativity, and long-term progression.

We Love Katamari

Ball-Rolling Sim : 2005

A whimsical action game where you roll a sticky ball (the Katamari) to collect objects and grow it to absurd sizes. Its surreal humor and escalating scale make every level a joyful, chaotic puzzle.

World of Warcraft: Classic

MMO-RPG : 2004

A recreation of WoW's original 2004 experience, emphasizing slower progression, social grouping, and world exploration. It brings back the challenge, community reliance, and nostalgic feel of early MMORPG design.
